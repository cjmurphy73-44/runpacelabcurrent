# FIN-001: Scenario-based commercial economics (draft, 2026-11-17)

**Status: planning model only.** No revenue, customer counts, CAC, LTV, churn, conversion, bank balances, cash runway or costs have been measured or imported. This is not an accounting ledger or financial advice.

Run `npx vitest run docs/finance/financialScenario.test.mjs` (or the repository's full `npm run test:ci`). The adjacent pure function has no SDK, credentials, network calls, or production dependencies. Its sample inputs are **illustrative hypothetical assumptions**, not actual TrainPaceLab results.

## Input definitions and limitations

- Opening and new paying customers: hypothetical cohort assumptions; fractional expected churn is retained rather than rounding to falsely imply observed people.
- Churn: monthly proportion of opening customers. New customers are assumed not to churn during the acquisition month.
- Revenue: monthly AUD price multiplied by average opening/closing paid customer count. This is a simplified accrual approximation, **not Stripe cash receipts**, GST accounting, refunds, proration or payment failures.
- Variable cost: AUD per average customer per month, encompassing assumed hosting, AI, provider, support and payment costs. Obtain measured provider bills and Stripe fee schedule before setting it.
- Fixed cost and acquisition spend: separate monthly AUD assumptions; no actual operating budget or advertising commitment is implied.
- Closing cash: opening hypothetical cash plus modelled net cash flow; excludes GST, tax, working capital, setup expenses and financing.
- CAC: acquisition spend divided by newly acquired paying customers; returns null when zero. This omits organic acquisition effort and attribution uncertainty.
- LTV: (monthly price minus variable cost) divided by monthly churn; returns null when churn is zero. It is an idealized steady-state contribution estimate, **not realized lifetime value**; excludes retention cohort shape, discounts, refunds and discount rate.

## Acceptance before commercial use

Replace assumptions with documented actuals only with owner authorization, verify Stripe test-mode lifecycle and server-side entitlements, model tier mix and GST/payment fees, track conversion and retention with consent, and reconcile cash with accounting records outside this repository. Do not use this model to enable billing, buy ads, publish forecasts or assert traction. Release HOLD remains in force.
