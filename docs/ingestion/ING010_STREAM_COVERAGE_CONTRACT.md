# ING-010 stream coverage provenance contract
This is a **pure, synthetic, fail-closed** helper for full-versus-stored telemetry coverage. It is deliberately not wired to COROS, Garmin, FIT uploads or WorkoutSession writes. A prefix-limited 84-minute recording cannot be described as complete, even if its first 3600 samples are valid. Stored count, full count, full and stored timestamp spans, and recording duration are required; invalid metadata throws rather than guessing.

Acceptance: `npx vitest run scripts/ingestion/streamCoverageProvenance.test.mjs`, repository typecheck/build/source-map guard. Integration remains blocked pending authorized chunked storage or authenticated original-asset processing and measured per-entity limits. Do not replace or truncate source FIT files; do not expose raw asset URLs or proprietary physiology algorithms. No migration or production writes. This is **not** resolution of #51 until the ingest writers and downstream displays consume validated provenance. Release HOLD.

**Coverage gate refinement (2026-11-19):** A verified stream may be eligible for analysis at a 60% duration threshold, but this is insufficient to label an entire recording *complete*. This isolated provenance helper now requires at least 95% measured duration span, matching first/last full timestamps and all recorded samples. The 95% tolerance is conservative and provisional; integration must reconcile device sampling cadence, start/end boundaries, and authoritative duration before displaying a full-recording claim. The helper cannot independently establish absence of interior gaps from aggregate metadata alone.
